Heritage & Leadership

Serving Construction Needs with Proven Trust

Established in 2016 in Gokavaram, Andhra Pradesh, VENKATARAMANA ENTERPRISES has built its foundation on delivering dependable building materials for homes, commercial infrastructure, and regional construction contractors.

Positioned opposite the Police Station on Main Road, we provide a genuine one-stop destination for cement, reinforcement steel, masonry blocks, and essential construction chemicals.
